Barcelona
July 15–17 · 2 nights
STL → PHL → BCN AA 2720 + AA 742 · Departs 11:20am Jul 14 · Arrives 6:45am Jul 15

Landing exhausted after an overnight flight — keep it simple. One iconic thing per day, lots of wandering, and don't stress about seeing everything. Barcelona rewards slowness.

Marbella
July 17–22 · 5 nights · Costa del Sol base

Your anchor. Five nights on the Costa del Sol with a car gives you serious flexibility — beach days, day trips, late lunches, early swims. Don't over-programme it. The Mediterranean in July is perfect.

Sevilla
July 22–25 · 3 nights

The most romantic and dramatic city in Spain. Enormous history, incredible food, and a pace that suits families. Remember: siesta is real here (2–5pm everything closes), dinner starts at 9pm, and tapas are free in some bars.

Madrid
July 25–28 · 3 nights · Fly home Jul 28

Two full days in the capital. Madrid is cooler than Sevilla, greener, and has a completely different energy. World-class museums, incredible food markets, and a park that could absorb a whole day. Relaxed final stretch before heading home.
